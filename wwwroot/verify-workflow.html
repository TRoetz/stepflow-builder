🎯 How to Build a Stepflow

  1. Add Steps: Drag step templates from sidebar (AI, SQL, API, RULE, PASS, SCRIPT, DUCKDB, HTTP)
  2. Position Steps: Drag steps to desired positions on canvas
  3. Connect Steps: Click "Connect →" button on a step to create connection lines
  4. Export Workflow: Click "📤 Export" to download workflow as JSON
